# Privacy Policy — Sudoku

_Last updated: 24 July 2026_

This policy covers the **Sudoku** app (package `com.sparklebit.sudoku`, "the App"),
published by **Sparkle Bit Games**.

## Data we collect

**Gameplay data (stored only on your device).** Puzzle progress, notes/pencil marks,
best times, daily-challenge streak, coin balance, and settings. This is stored locally
on your device via Android DataStore/SharedPreferences and is never sent to us.
Uninstalling the App deletes it.

**Advertising data (via Google AdMob).** The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob
and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP
address, and app-interaction data to serve and measure ads, and — where you consent —
to personalize them. In the EEA, UK, and applicable US states, ads are requested
without personalization unless you consent through the in-app consent form shown when
you first open the App. This collection is governed by
[Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Analytics (via Google Firebase Analytics).** The App collects anonymous usage events
(for example, which screens are opened and when a puzzle is completed) to help us
understand how the App is used and improve it. In regions where consent is required,
analytics collection is enabled only after you consent through the in-app consent form.

**Crash diagnostics (via Google Firebase Crashlytics).** If the App crashes, we collect
a diagnostic report (such as the stack trace, device model, and operating-system
version) so we can fix the problem. These reports do not identify you personally.

## What we do NOT collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any
other directly identifying information. We operate no server of our own — all gameplay
data stays on your device.

## Your choices

- **Change or withdraw ad-personalization consent** at any time from
  **Settings → Privacy options** inside the App.
- **Reset all local data** by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal
data from children, and ads are requested without personalization unless age/consent
signals indicate otherwise, in line with AdMob's policies.
